# E-Commerce Business Analytics Dashboard

Interactive business analytics project built using **SQL** and **Power BI** to analyze revenue trends, customer behavior, operational efficiency, cancellations, delays, and delivery performance in an e-commerce environment.

---

## Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming raw e-commerce order data into actionable business insights through SQL-based analysis and Power BI dashboarding.

The dashboard helps identify:
- Revenue performance trends
- Customer order behavior
- Cancellation and delay patterns
- Peak operational hours
- Customer lifetime value insights
- SLA and delivery efficiency gaps

## Key Business Analysis Performed

### 1. Revenue vs Cancellation Analysis
Analyzed monthly revenue trends against cancellation percentages to identify customer purchase confidence and operational efficiency patterns.

### 2. Peak Hour & Delay Analysis
Studied order volume across hourly intervals and compared delay percentages to identify operational bottlenecks during peak and non-peak hours.

### 3. SLA & Delivery Performance Monitoring
Measured delayed order percentage against SLA targets to evaluate delivery efficiency and fulfillment quality.

### 4. Revenue Contribution by City
Compared city-wise revenue contribution to identify high-performing markets and revenue concentration patterns.

### 5. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Analysis
Performed customer-level revenue and order analysis using SQL ranking and aggregation techniques to identify valuable and loyal customers.

---

## Key Insights

- Higher revenue months showed lower cancellation percentages, indicating stronger customer purchase confidence.
- Delay percentages did not always increase during peak order hours, suggesting operational inefficiencies beyond order load alone.
- Bangalore contributed the highest share of revenue among analyzed cities.
- Delay percentage exceeded SLA target, indicating opportunities for logistics and operational improvement.
- Delivered orders accounted for the majority of transactions, though cancellation and delay rates highlighted customer experience risks.
